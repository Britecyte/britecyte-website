A Breakthrough in Adipose Science.

A new era of adipose restoration begins with technology that makes native adipose tissue available when and where it's needed.

  1. 01

    Native Structure & Function Preserved

    Britecyte's proprietary processing technology preserves the native architecture of adipose tissue, including intact adipocytes and extracellular matrix, maintaining the structural and functional characteristics that make adipose tissue unique.

  2. 02

    Immunogenic Components Removed

    The technology selectively removes immunogenic components that have historically limited adipose tissue to autologous use, enabling allogeneic transplantation while preserving native tissue architecture.

  3. 03

    Ready When You Need It

    Unlike fresh autologous fat, our technology allows for convenient storage and immediate clinical use. Stable refrigerated storage (0–10°C) simplifies logistics while providing an off-the-shelf solution when patients need treatment.

  4. 04

    Designed for Minimally Invasive Delivery

    The preserved tissue is formulated for minimally invasive implantation using standard cannulas or needles, allowing physicians to restore lost structure through a familiar clinical workflow.
